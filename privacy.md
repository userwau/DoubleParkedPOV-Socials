# Privacy Policy for DoubleParkedPOV

**Last Updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DoubleParkedPOV ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our services and applications that integrate with the TikTok Developer API.

---

### 1. Information We Access
When you authenticate your TikTok account with DoubleParkedPOV, we only request the permissions necessary to operate our integration. Depending on the authorized scopes, this may include:
- **Public Profile Information:** Display name, avatar image, and unique identifiers (open ID / union ID).
- **Video & Media Content:** Upload metadata, video URLs, and captions strictly for publishing or managing content on your authorized account.

We do not collect passwords, payment details, or direct messages.

---

### 2. How We Use Information
The information accessed through TikTok APIs is used solely to:
- Authenticate and verify your account credentials.
- Enable automated publishing, scheduling, or content synchronization for your TikTok profile.
- Maintain application reliability and prevent misuse.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your account data with third parties or data brokers.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security
- Any authentication credentials (such as OAuth access and refresh tokens) are securely handled and kept confidential.
- We retain tokens and authorization data only for as long as necessary to provide service functionality or until authorization is revoked.

---

### 4. Data Deletion and Revocation
You retain full control over your data:
- **Revoke TikTok Access:** You can disconnect DoubleParkedPOV at any time via your TikTok account under **Settings and privacy** > **Security & permissions** > **Manage app permissions**.
- **Data Deletion Requests:** To request the deletion of any stored tokens or metadata associated with your account, please open an issue or contact us via our GitHub repository at [https://github.com/userwau/DoubleParkedPOV-Socials](https://github.com/userwau/DoubleParkedPOV-Socials). Deletion requests are processed promptly.
